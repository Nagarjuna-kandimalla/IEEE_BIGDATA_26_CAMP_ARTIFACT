\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Workflow memory prediction.}
Prior work estimates task requirements from prelaunch inputs and completed
tasks~\cite{bader2025overview}. Ferreira da Silva et al.
\cite{dasilva2013online,dasilva2015online} update runtime, disk, and peak-memory
estimates as tasks complete; Tovar et al. choose historical allocations or
reshape splittable analyses~\cite{tovar2018jobsizing,tovar2022shaping}; and Witt
et al. optimize allocation cost using online feedback
\cite{witt2019feedback,witt2019icecube}. Sizey trains and reselects multiple
models through resource-allocation quality~\cite{bader2024sizey}; Ponder selects
task-sizing strategies~\cite{lehmann2024ponder}. Related methods predict
time-varying memory~\cite{bader2023dynamic}, learn allocation actions
\cite{bader2022reinforcement}, profile runtime cold starts
\cite{bader2024lotaru}, or learn from scheduler logs
\cite{rodrigues2016helping,tanash2019slurm}. CAMP instead changes allocator
information by separating staged size from selectively measured consumption
and admitting only completed history. Its external experiment preserves
Sizey's implementation rather than treating it as a feature ablation.

\textbf{Tracing and provenance.}
System-call and Linux audit detail has workload-dependent cost
\cite{zeng2016linux}. LPM~\cite{bates2015trustworthy} and
CamFlow~\cite{pasquier2017practical} capture whole-system causal provenance.
eBPF executes verified programs at kernel hooks
\cite{gbadamosi2024ebpf}; CAMP uses it more narrowly for
task-attributed positive reads and file-backed mmap faults, not complete syscall
lineage. Standard and audit lanes therefore make measurement coverage an
allocation decision.

\textbf{Budgeted measurement.}
Active learning selects labels under measurement limits
\cite{settles2009active}. CAMP analogously observes peak RSS for every task but
consumption only for selected tasks, combining risk/information exploitation
with process-aware exploration at exact budgets. Nonuniform selection can bias
history: the random lane records inclusion probabilities and applies capped
Horvitz--Thompson weights~\cite{horvitz1952generalization}. This reduces bias
for stochastic exploration but does not make deterministic risk/information
selections a probability sample; CAMP therefore claims selection yield, not an
unbiased complete audit set.
