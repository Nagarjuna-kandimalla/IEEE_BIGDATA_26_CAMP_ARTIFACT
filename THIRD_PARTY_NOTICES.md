# Third-party material and inherited licensing

- The standalone Ponder module contains original Common Workflow Scheduler
  source and new adaptation code under GPL-3.0-or-later. See its
  [notice](SCRIPTS/BASELINES/ponder/NOTICE.md),
  [license](SCRIPTS/BASELINES/ponder/COPYING), and source manifest.
- IEEEtran class/style files retain their embedded author and license notices.
- Inherited Sizey, model libraries, workflow integrations, and other vendor code
  retain their existing attribution and licensing information. Upstream software
  acquired by the reproduction scripts remains subject to its own license.
- The source CAMP repository did not supply a root license file. This packaging
  does not invent one or relicense the full artifact. Author licensing decisions
  for original CAMP material remain separate from third-party notices.

Research papers and figures remain attributed to their authors. Cite the accepted
WORKS work for reused evidence and distinguish it from newly performed Big Data
experiments. Bibliographic metadata is preserved in the manuscript's bibliography.
